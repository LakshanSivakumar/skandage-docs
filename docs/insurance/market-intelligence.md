# Market Intelligence

You shouldn't need to open a browser mid-meeting to answer "how are the markets doing." Skandage keeps a live view of global markets built in.

## 1. World Indexes

Live quotes for 20 major world indexes, grouped by region:
* **Americas:** S&P 500, Dow Jones, Nasdaq, Russell 2000, TSX, Bovespa
* **Europe:** FTSE 100, DAX, CAC 40, Euro Stoxx 50, IBEX 35, FTSE MIB
* **Asia-Pacific:** Nikkei 225, Hang Seng, Shanghai Composite, KOSPI, ASX 200, Sensex, Nifty 50, and the **Straits Times Index**

## 2. Sector Heatmap

A treemap of the full S&P 500, grouped into the 11 GICS sectors. Tile size is weighted by market cap, and tile color reflects price change over a range you choose — anywhere from 1 day out to 10 years. Drill into any sector to see its individual constituents (the top-level view caps at the 10 largest per sector to stay readable), with company logos shown for the larger names.

## 3. Fund Alerts

Covered in [Fund Explorer](./fund-explorer#6-fund-alerts) — set a target NAV on a specific fund and get notified once it's crossed.
