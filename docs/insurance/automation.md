# PDF Parsing & Summaries

Manual data entry is dead. The Skandage parsing engine reads official insurer PDFs and instantly extracts the data, allowing you to generate summaries in seconds instead of hours.

## 1. Automatic Summary Generator
Consolidating a client's portfolio used to take hours. Now, it takes one click.

1. Navigate to the **Summary** tab.
2. Drag and drop your client's Prudential policy PDFs into the window.
3. **The Magic:** The system will automatically read the documents and output a clean, formatted Excel summary.
4. **Smart Appending:** If your client already has an existing Excel summary with older policies or policies from other insurers, just upload it! Skandage will intelligently append *only* the new Prudential policies to the existing sheet without overwriting their history.

## 2. Prospecting Engine
When taking over a prospect, you need to know exactly what they already have.
* Drop in their existing **Prudential or AIA PDFs**.
* The system parses their current coverage and creates a profile. You can manually input crucial parameters like their Age, Income, and Housing Loan details to instantly spot coverage gaps.

## 3. Protection & Wealth Reports
If you have generated a Sales Quotation System (SQS) PDF for a client, don't just hand them a raw, confusing document.
* Go to the **Protection** or **Wealth** tabs.
* Upload the SQS PDF. 
* Skandage will digest the numbers and spit out a beautifully branded, easy-to-understand advisory report that you can present to close the case.
