# The Client Vault & Map

Your database is the lifeblood of your business. The Insurance Suite makes it effortless to import, edit, and visualize your entire client roster.

## 1. Importing Your Clients
You don't need to start from scratch. We support direct imports so you can migrate your book of business in seconds.

* **Via Pruplanner:** Export your client list from Pruplanner and drag the file directly into the Skandage **Clients** tab. 
* **Via CSV:** Have your own Excel tracker? Use our standard CSV template to map your columns and upload.
* **Web Sync:** The desktop app integrates tightly with your Skandage Website Suite, pulling down any new leads captured from your online Gap Calculator.

## 2. Editing Client Dossiers
Click on any client to open their Dossier. Here you can edit their contact info, marital status, and attach specific tags (e.g., `HNW`, `SME Owner`). 

## 3. The Map Feature (Routing)
Stop wasting time crisscrossing the island for appointments. The built-in Map feature plots every client's address visually.

* **Radius Search:** Going to Tampines to meet a client? Open the map and search that location to instantly see which other clients live nearby.
* **Route Optimization:** Ping those nearby clients to stack your appointments in the same area and save hours of travel time.

> **Skandage Tip:** When a client updates their address in your CRM, the Map automatically repositions their pin. Always ask for updated addresses during your annual reviews!
