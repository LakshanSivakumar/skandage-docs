# Advisory & Engagement

Once the data is parsed, it is time to build the strategy and maintain the relationship.

## 1. The Retirement Planner
Calculating a realistic retirement timeline requires heavy math. Let Skandage do it.
1. Go to the **Retirement** tab.
2. Select an existing client from your database, or upload their policy summary.
3. Input their target retirement age, desired monthly payout, and inflation assumptions.
4. Click **Export**. The system will generate a comprehensive Retirement Projection in either PDF or Excel format, ready for your presentation.

## 2. Engagement (Automated CRM)
Relationships drive renewals and referrals. The Engagement tab ensures you never drop the ball.
* **Review Reminders:** See exactly who is due for their annual policy review this month.
* **Birthday & Festive Cards:** The system tracks dates of birth and major holidays, allowing you to queue up personalized greeting cards directly from your desktop.

## 3. Performance Tracking
You cannot manage what you do not measure. 
* Open the **Performance** tab to view your personal agent dashboard. 
* Track your API (Annual Premium Income), case counts, and active client metrics to ensure you are on pace for MDRT or your agency targets.
