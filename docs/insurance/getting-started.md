# Getting Started

## Installing

Skandage runs natively on **macOS** (Intel and Apple Silicon) and **Windows 10/11**, plus a companion app on **iOS**. Download the installer for your platform from [skandage.com/download](https://skandage.com/download.html) and open it.

## Activating

The first screen you'll see asks you to activate the app, either with a subscription or an offline license key — pick whichever matches how you bought it.

### Option A: Subscribe

If Skandage is available as a subscription on your platform, tap **Subscribe to Skandage** and choose **Monthly** or **Annual** (the annual plan is flagged as best value). Payment goes through your platform's own store, so Skandage never sees or stores your card details. Already subscribed on another device? Use **Restore Purchases** to unlock the app without paying again.

### Option B: Activation Key

If you bought a license key directly, switch to key entry and paste it into the **License Key** field, then tap **Activate Product**.

A couple of things worth knowing about how the key works:
* It's tied to *this specific device* — generated from your machine's own ID, which is shown on screen so you can quote it to support if something goes wrong.
* It's checked entirely offline. There's no license server to reach, so activation works even on a machine that's never touched the internet.
* One key covers one device. Moving to a new laptop means activating there with the same key; if you're not sure whether that's still valid, reach out to [support@skandage.com](mailto:support@skandage.com).

## First Launch

Once activated, open **Settings** and fill in your **Agent Profile** (display name and agency/organization name) — this is what appears on your Proposal Engine cover pages and printed reports, so it's worth getting right before you generate anything for a client.

From there, head to **[The Client Vault](./database)** to bring in your existing book of business.
