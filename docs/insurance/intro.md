# The Insurance Suite OS

Welcome to your new digital headquarters.

While the Skandage Website Suite handles your public-facing marketing and lead capture, the **Insurance Suite** is your private, offline desktop and mobile operating system. It's built to handle the actual work of running an advisory practice: tracking your book of clients, planning appointments, modelling CPF and retirement numbers, comparing funds and ILP schemes, and putting together a client-ready proposal — without any of it leaving your device.

**100% Local. 100% Secure.**
Your client database is a single encrypted SQLite file (`.skandb`) that lives on your own hard drive, not on a Skandage server. Nothing about a client's name, NRIC, or policy details is ever uploaded anywhere unless *you* choose to sync it — see [Sync & Backup](./sync-backup) for how that works if you use more than one device.

---

### Core Modules

* **[Client Vault](./database)** — Your entire book, imported from PDFs, CSV, or Excel, with a guided duplicate-merge tool so nothing gets messy.
* **[Map](./map)** — See every client on a live map of Singapore, clustered and sorted by distance from wherever you're looking.
* **[CRM & Engagement](./crm)** — Birthday and policy-review reminders, plus one-click WhatsApp broadcasts.
* **[Proposal Engine](./proposal-engine)** — A twelve-module suite covering everything from the cover page to CPF retirement modelling to a premium financing simulator, built to produce one client-ready pack.
* **[Fund Explorer](./fund-explorer)** — Live NAV tracking across multiple insurers, XIRR calculations, and a fund switch simulator for "what if we'd switched" conversations.
* **[ILP Comparison](./ilp-comparison)** — Line up two ILP schemes side by side across six insurers and adjust assumptions independently.
* **[Market Intelligence](./market-intelligence)** — World indexes, a full S&P 500 sector heatmap, and fund price alerts, all inside the app.
