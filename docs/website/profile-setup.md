# Profile Configuration

Your Skandage Site is not a digital brochure; it is a conversion engine. The Profile Configuration dictates the core identity variables that populate across your chosen framework.

Get these details right, and your site will immediately project enterprise-level trust.

---

## 1. Core Identity
Navigate to **Dashboard > Profile Settings**. This is where you establish your digital baseline.

* **Display Name:** Use your professional name as registered with your agency. 
* **Professional Title:** Be specific. Instead of "Agent," use "Senior Wealth Manager" or "Financial Consultant."
* **Agency Affiliation:** Input your respective agency or firm. *Note: Ensure your inputs comply with your internal compliance guidelines regarding public affiliations.*

> **Skandage Tip:** > Keep your **Biography** under 3 short paragraphs. High-net-worth prospects skim. Focus on *who* you help and *how* you protect their wealth, not just a list of products you sell.

## 2. Professional Credentials
Trust is verified through credentials. You can add unlimited certifications to your profile, which will automatically format into badges on your live site.

1. Go to **Dashboard > Credentials**.
2. Click **+ Add Credential**.
3. Input the credential (e.g., `MDRT 2025`, `ChFC®`, `AWP™`).
4. Reorder them using the drag-and-drop handles to prioritize your highest achievements.

## 3. Visual Assets
Your headshot is the anchor of your digital presence. 

* **The Upload:** Navigate to the **Media** section of your profile.
* **Format:** Upload a high-resolution `.jpg` or `.png`. 
* **Best Practices:** We highly recommend a studio-quality portrait with a transparent or clean, solid-color background. Because Skandage Sites support both Light and Dark modes dynamically, a transparent background (`.png`) ensures your headshot looks flawless regardless of the user's system theme.

## 4. Contact Vectors
How prospects reach you is critical.

* **Direct Email:** Your primary professional email.
* **WhatsApp Link:** We automatically generate a secure `wa.me` routing link based on your registered mobile number.
* **Office Location:** (Optional) Add your agency branch address if you conduct physical client meetings.
