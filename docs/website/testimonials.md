# Client Testimonials

In financial advisory, trust is your only real currency. 

Before a prospect ever uses your Gap Calculator or clicks the WhatsApp button to book a meeting, they will look for proof that you actually deliver on your promises. Having a dedicated section for 5-star client reviews is the fastest way to turn a skeptical visitor into a warm lead.

Here is how to manage your "Social Proof" engine on Skandage.

---

## 1. Adding a Testimonial Manually
If a client recently texted you a glowing review or sent you a thank-you email, you can easily copy and paste it right onto your site.

1. Go to your **Dashboard** and select **Testimonials** (or Reviews).
2. Click the **+ Add Testimonial** button.
3. **The Details:** Enter your client's name. (If privacy is a concern, using initials like "Mr. J.L." is perfectly acceptable).
4. **The Title:** Add their profession or relationship to you (e.g., "Business Owner," "Client of 5 Years," or "Senior Executive"). This helps prospects relate to your existing clients.
5. **The Quote:** Paste their review and click **Save**.

## 2. Generating a Review Link (The Easy Way)
Chasing clients for reviews is awkward. We built a feature to do the heavy lifting for you.

Instead of typing reviews manually, you can send your clients a direct link to submit their own feedback.

1. In your **Testimonials** dashboard, click **Generate Review Link**.
2. Copy the secure link provided.
3. Send this link to your clients via WhatsApp or Email after a successful policy review or claim payout. 

When your client fills out the form, their review will automatically be routed to your dashboard for your approval before it goes live.

## 3. Highlighting Your Best Reviews
Not all reviews are created equal. You want your absolute best success stories featured prominently on your homepage.

* In your Testimonials list, you will see a **Feature** toggle (usually a star icon) next to each review.
* Click this toggle to instantly pin that specific review to the front page of your Skandage Site. 
* Keep your featured reviews fresh by rotating them every few months.

> **Skandage Tip:** The most powerful testimonials are specific. A review that says, *"He helped me restructure my portfolio to save $500 a month"* will always convert more prospects than one that simply says, *"Great agent, very friendly."* If a client leaves a vague review, it is okay to politely ask if you can add a specific metric they achieved with your help!
