# Publishing Articles & SEO

In the financial advisory space, clients do not want to be sold to; they want to be educated. 

Having a "Blog" or an "Articles" section on your Skandage Site is how you transition from being seen as a salesperson to being respected as a subject matter expert. It proves you understand the complexities of wealth accumulation, CPF changes, and market trends.

More importantly, publishing articles is the single best way to get your website to show up on Google.

---

## The "Why" Behind Articles

Writing a short, 500-word article once a month provides massive leverage for your business.

1. **The "Soft Touch" Follow-Up:** Instead of texting a cold prospect, *"Hi, are you ready to meet?"*, you can send them a link to your site saying, *"Hi, I just wrote a quick breakdown on the new CPF Life changes, thought of you and wanted to share."* It adds value without being pushy.
2. **Pre-Selling:** When a prospect reads your thoughts on early retirement planning before they meet you, they walk into the meeting already trusting your methodology.

## Why This is Good for SEO

**SEO (Search Engine Optimization)** is just a technical term for "showing up on Google when someone searches for a solution."

Google's entire job is to serve the most helpful answers to its users. If your website only has a homepage and a contact form, Google has nothing to show people. 

However, if you publish an article titled *"How to maximize your SRS account in 2026,"* and a high-net-worth prospect in Singapore searches that exact question, Google will index your article and put your Skandage Site in front of them for free. 
* **Every article you write is a new hook in the ocean.** The more hooks you have, the more organic leads you will catch.

---

## How to Publish & Broadcast on Skandage

We built the Article engine to be dead simple, and we included a superpower: one-click broadcasting.

### 1. Writing the Article
1. Go to your **Dashboard** and select **Articles**.
2. Click **+ New Article**.
3. Add a catchy title, write your content in the editor, and click **Publish**. It will instantly appear on your live website.

### 2. The Broadcast Feature (Your Superpower)
You don't have to wait for people to find your article on Google. Skandage allows you to push your expertise directly to your audience.

Once your article is published, you will see two action buttons next to it in your dashboard:
* **Email to All:** Instantly formats your article into a clean newsletter and sends it to every lead you have captured.
* **WhatsApp to All:** Generates a clean broadcast message with a link to your article, ready to be sent to your client lists.

> **Skandage Tip:** Don't know what to write about? Answer the questions your clients ask you every single day. If three clients ask you about "Shield Plans" in a week, write an article called *"The 3 Things Most People Misunderstand About Shield Plans."* If they are asking the question in person, thousands of people are asking Google the exact same thing.
