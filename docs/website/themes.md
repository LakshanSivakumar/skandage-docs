# Choosing Your Look & Layout

First impressions matter. The design you choose for your Skandage Site tells your prospects exactly what kind of financial consultant you are before they even read a word. 

The best part? You don't need to know anything about web design. You can completely change the look, feel, and structure of your entire website with a few simple clicks.

---

## 1. Pick Your Colors
Every brand is different. Whether you want to match your specific agency's colors or create your own unique personal brand, we have you covered.

We offer several **Standard Themes** that instantly update the color scheme of your buttons, accents, and backgrounds. 
* Want a sleek, professional dark mode? We have that.
* Prefer a clean, bright, and modern white background? Just click and switch.
* Need a specific accent color (like red, blue, or gold) to match your business cards? You can select that, too.

## 2. Pick Your Layout
Design isn't just about colors; it's about how your information is organized. 

Skandage Sites come with different **Layouts** so you can decide what your clients see first:
* **The "Front and Center" Layout:** Puts your professional headshot and name right in the middle of the screen. Great for building strong personal recognition.
* **The "Side-by-Side" Layout:** Places your image on one side and your professional biography on the other. Excellent for agents who want to immediately highlight their credentials and experience.

---

## How to Switch Your Design

Changing your site's look is instant and won't delete any of your written content, reviews, or links.

1. Go to your **Dashboard** and navigate to your **Site Settings**.
2. Look for the **Appearance** or **Theme** area.
3. Pick your favorite Layout and Color combination.
4. Click **Save**.

Your live website will instantly update to the new look.

> **Skandage Tip:** Don't overthink it! Pick the design that matches the type of clients you *want* to attract. You can always log back in and change it later in two seconds if you change your mind.
